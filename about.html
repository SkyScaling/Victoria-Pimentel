Victoria Pimentel

Principal, Client Advisory · Legacy Capital Life

Hi, I'm Victoria. I work with business owners across Ontario who have built something real and want to keep more of what their business earns.

A lot of business owners are in the same spot. Money builds up in the corporation and gets taxed. RRSPs max out fast. And there's no real plan if they get sick or hurt and can't work. My job is to show you if there's a better way to handle all three.

I came to this from an unusual direction. For eight years I worked in HR focusing on return-to-work, accommodations and benefits. My job was the thing nobody plans for: when someone gets sick, gets hurt, or can't come in, I sat with them and their employer and figured out what they were actually entitled to. Too often people find out where they stand only when it's already too late.

That's why I joined Legacy Capital Life at the founders' invitation, to bring that practical experience into financial advice and have the conversation before something happens. I'm a Principal of the firm, our Chief Compliance Officer, and I hold my LLQP. I take complicated things like corporate money and insurance and turn them into clear, honest guidance you can act on.

I'm not interested in one-off transactions. I want a long relationship built on trust and transparency, where you know exactly what you have and why you have it. And I work with your accountant, not around them, so everything fits together.

I live just outside Hamilton in Binbrook, Ontario, and I'm a mom of two boys. Most weekends you'll find us at a baseball game.

On our call I'll ask how your business is doing, where your money sits today, how you pay yourself, and what happens if you can't work. Then I'll show you, in plain English, if there's a better way to grow it, retire on it and protect it. If what you have already works, I'll tell you and let you get on with your day.

My aim is for you to hang up feeling confident, understood and clear on your next step. No pressure, no jargon, no obligation.
