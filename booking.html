Three Things I Look At With Business Owners
You don't need all three. On the call, we focus on the ones that matter to you.
1

The money sitting in your company

Instead of leaving it all in regular investments, part of it can go into an insurance policy owned by your corporation. It grows without a yearly tax bill, you can borrow against it, and later most of it can pass to your family tax-free.

2

Your retirement

RRSPs have a yearly limit, and if you earn well, you hit it fast. The same policy can help pay for your retirement. Instead of cashing it out and paying tax, you can borrow against it from a bank while it keeps growing.

3

Your income if you get sick or hurt

Some plans stop paying if you can do any job at all, even one that pays far less. A policy built around your own job pays you if you can't do your work. With the right option, if you stay healthy, part of what you paid can come back to you.

Have employees? I can also look at your group benefits and see if your business is paying more than it should.

General information only. What fits depends on your business, your health and your accountant's advice. That's what the 15-minute call is for.

Questions, Answered
The things business owners ask me most before they book.

Q.Is this really free?

Yes. The call costs you nothing and there's no obligation. Booking a time doesn't sign you up for anything.

Q.Do I need a lot of money in my corporation?

There's no set amount. Some of this works best once your business has money left over each year. On the call I'll tell you honestly if it fits you now, later, or not at all.

Q.Will this replace my accountant?

No. Your accountant handles your taxes. I handle the insurance side. I work with them so everything fits together.

Q.Why not wait until later?

Most of this uses insurance, and you can only get it while you're healthy. The sooner you start, the more time your money has to grow.

Q.Will I be pressured to buy something?

No. My job is to show you your options and answer your questions in plain language. If it's a fit, great. If not, no hard feelings.

Q.How long is it, and do I need to prepare?

About 15 minutes on the phone, at a time you pick. Just answer the phone. If you know roughly how much is sitting in your corporation, that helps, but it's not needed.
